Simple content formatting

Paste ordinary notes or use headings to decide where slides begin.

## Slide title or Slide 1 — Title
Starts a new slide.
### Subheading
Groups points inside a slide.
- Your point
Adds a point. Indent a point to nest it.
**important words**
Makes those words bold.

Long sections may continue on another slide to keep the text readable. For AI content, aim for 2–5 short points, titles under 60 characters, and no more than 60 words per slide. Tables work best with 3 columns, 4 data rows, and short cells. Larger tables are measured and split automatically.
